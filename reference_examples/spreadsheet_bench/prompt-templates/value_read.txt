- An input cell your answer depends on may itself hold an Excel formula. A plain
  `load_workbook("input.xlsx")` returns that cell's raw formula **text** (`"=..."`), or

  producing a wrong answer even when your transformation logic is correct.
- To read the **computed value** of any input cell, load a second workbook with
  `data_only=True` and read from it (this is the same value you see when you explore).
- Still SAVE the workbook you loaded *without* `data_only` (a `data_only=True` workbook
  drops other cells' formulas on save). So: read values from the `data_only=True` book,
  write your literal answers into the normal book, and save the normal book.
